These are **signals, not verdicts**. A flagged line can be exactly his voice (the "Ma perché cazzo è blu" heading in the Phillip Lim piece is).

## The loop (5 to 10 minutes a piece)

1. Read the piece once, out loud in your head. Mark what does not sound like you.
2. Fix only voice: cut the repeated word, the explainer heading, the recap line. Keep every fact.
3. Anything that needs a detail you did not give, ask for the detail. Do not fill it.
4. `npm run build`, look at it, then `npx wrangler deploy` (personal account, check `npx wrangler whoami` first).

## What to look for

- **A heading that could sit on any article.** "La verità" is the section heading in five: Plein, Tory Burch, KCD, Zimmermann, Jil Sander. Replace each with the concrete thing that section is about ("Il fuoco", "Il coro", the way Pyer Moss became "Il coro").
- **"Perché ... funziona / è" explainer headings.** Y/Project ("Perché funzionava"), Serafini, the raw-files piece.
- **Press-release phrases:** "celebrazione", "mette in scena", "trasformando", "uno dei momenti forti". They say nothing you saw.
- **"Non X. Y." pairs** and a closing line that repeats the whole piece.
- **One abstract word said five times.** (Stella said "convinzione" six.)
- **A piece that is thin** next to its neighbours and has no detail from you.
